**Learnings**
- TikTok carousels with concrete *what to say / what to do with the body* scripts are the strongest format (≈1,000 views each); generic habit/routine lists (habits, morning reset, Sunday) stall under 250.
- On Instagram carousels get almost no reach (≤8 views) with 3 followers — reels are the only IG growth lever for now.
- Reels that hold attention open on a concrete situation or a short claim (eyes, slow pace, the text after a date); breathwork and long story set-ups ("he used to…") lose viewers in the first 3 s (skip ≥0.8).
- 30-Day Rebuild: on TikTok Days 2–4 hold ~700 views each (Day 1 was not distributed: 1 view); on IG it is the weakest slot (5–49 views, skip 0.73–1.0). Day 3/4 data is still young.
- Several TikTok posts got almost no For You traffic (For You share ≤0.57: wed 15:34/18:34, thu 09:35, fri 12:34/15:46/18:30 → 1–52 views). Content-independent; worth watching whether the 3-in-a-row pattern on Friday repeats.
